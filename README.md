# The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)
### A Movie Review

## A. Introduction

**The Amazing Spider-Man** is a superhero movie released in 2012 and directed by Marc Webb. It tells the story of Peter Parker, an ordinary high school student who gains extraordinary abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider. After discovering the truth about his parents, Peter begins exploring his new powers and eventually becomes the superhero known as Spider-Man.

The movie combines **action, adventure, science fiction, and romance**. It shows how Peter learns to use his abilities while facing personal problems and protecting the people he cares about.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

## B. Synopsis

The story follows Peter Parker, a high school student who lives with his Uncle Ben and Aunt May after his parents mysteriously disappear when he is young. One day, Peter discovers a briefcase belonging to his father and begins investigating his parents' past.

His investigation leads him to Oscorp, where he meets Dr. Curt Connors, a scientist who once worked with Peter's father. While exploring the laboratory, Peter is bitten by a genetically modified spider and develops incredible abilities, including enhanced strength, agility, and the ability to climb walls.

As Peter learns to control his new powers, he becomes closer to Gwen Stacy, a fellow student whose father is a police captain. However, Peter's life takes a dangerous turn when Dr. Connors transforms into a giant reptilian creature known as the Lizard.

The Lizard plans to release a chemical substance that would transform humans into reptilian creatures. Peter realizes that he must use his abilities to stop Connors and protect New York City.

Throughout the movie, Peter faces difficult choices, experiences personal loss, and learns that being a hero means accepting responsibility for his actions.

## C. Characters

Here are five important characters from the movie:

1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** – The main character who gains superhuman abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider. He becomes Spider-Man and uses his powers to protect innocent people.

2. **Gwen Stacy** – A smart and talented student who works at Oscorp. She becomes Peter's girlfriend and supports him throughout his journey.

3. **Dr. Curt Connors / The Lizard** – A scientist who researches genetic regeneration. His experiments turn him into a dangerous reptilian creature.

4. **Uncle Ben Parker** – Peter's uncle and guardian who teaches him important lessons about responsibility and doing what is right.

5. **Aunt May Parker** – Peter's caring aunt who raises him and supports him through his struggles.

## D. Personal Review

I chose **The Amazing Spider-Man** because Spider-Man is one of my favorite superheroes. I like how the movie shows Peter Parker's journey from an ordinary teenager into someone who accepts the responsibility of being a hero. It also has an interesting combination of action, romance, and emotional moments.

### Three Things I Like About the Movie

- **Action Scenes:** The fight scenes and Spider-Man's movements make the movie exciting and entertaining.
- **Character Development:** Peter Parker's transformation and his struggles with responsibility make his character interesting and relatable.
- **Visual Effects:** The special effects, web-swinging scenes, and appearance of the Lizard make the movie enjoyable to watch.

### Personal Rating

**My Rating: 10/10**

I would give The Amazing Spider-Man a 10 out of 10 because of its exciting action scenes, interesting characters, and emotional storyline. Andrew Garfield's performance also makes Peter Parker enjoyable to watch.

### Recommendation

I highly recommend The Amazing Spider-Man to people who enjoy superhero movies, action, and adventure. It is a great movie for Spider-Man fans and viewers who want to see a different interpretation of Peter Parker's origin story.
